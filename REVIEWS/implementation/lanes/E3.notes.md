## F-M-SUP M-35 (done)
- run() takes a `log: &dyn Fn(&str)`; Dispatcher logs via complain!("alert hook: {line}").
- Hook spawned with process_group(0); timeout does kill(-pgid, SIGKILL). Side effect: Ctrl-C on an interactive `watch` no longer reaches a running hook directly (it still dies at its deadline).
- After the hook exits, stderr/stdin threads get a 1 s grace; a background process the hook left holding a pipe is not waited on (thread detached) — deliberate, so `notifier &` in a hook does not block the next alert.
