The helper parts of `F-H29-fix-command-quoting.md` (`shell_quote`) and of `F-M-OUT-untrusted-text.md` (`display_safe`).

- Nothing else. The call sites belong to later lanes.

## Details

- **`shell_quote(&str) -> String`.** POSIX single-quote quoting, with `'` becoming `'\''`. Also a `shell_join(&[&str]) -> String`.
- **`display_safe(&str) -> Cow<str>`.** Replace C0 and C1 control characters, ESC, BEL and DEL with visible escapes such as `\x1b` and `\n`. Borrow when nothing changes.
- **`cap_line(&str, max_bytes) -> Cow<str>`.** Truncate on a UTF-8 boundary, with a `…` marker.
- **Tests:**
  - quoted output round-trips through `sh -c 'printf %s …'` for strings with quotes, `$()`, backticks, newlines and spaces;
  - a proptest checks that quoting then evaluating always returns the input;
  - `display_safe` never lets through a byte below 0x20, nor 0x7f, nor C1.
