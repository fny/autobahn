## Summary of stage 2

Resolution currently retires the losing copy and relies on the next cycle. That can't express "make every side this version" when the winner has not changed since the ancestor. Stage 1 refuses that case. Stage 2 makes it work:

- **Forget the path in the ancestor.** Besides retiring the loser, resolution removes the path from the ancestor, through a journal record. The next cycle then sees the winner as a creation, and propagates it everywhere: files, symlinks and directories alike. For `--keep both`, the renamed copy is a creation too.
- **Go through the owner.** The ancestor belongs to the session worker. When a supervisor is running, send the resolution over the control socket as a new request, and have the worker apply it between cycles, holding its own lock: retire the loser, forget the path, run a cycle. When no supervisor is running, `resolve` takes the session lock itself and writes the journal record.
- **One-way modes:** keeping beta stays refused.
- **Fan-out:** when one of several betas wins, every session in the group forgets the path.
- **Remove stage 1's refusals** that stage 2 makes unnecessary: the "winner still matches the ancestor" refusal. Keep the root refusal and the one-way refusal.

## Tests

The ticket's stage 2 tests:
- every mode and winner, on an already-agreed file, a real conflict and a directory, correct over three more cycles;
- a resolve sent while a cycle runs is applied after it;
- with fan-out, the other betas end with the winner's version;
- a mutation check that disabling the forget step brings back stage 1's deleted-winner bug.
