## Tickets
- T2-1: **done**, 6de14b7. A scan delta's length is capped at 4 GiB and pre-allocation at 8 MiB, the block size is range-checked when there is a baseline, and every op is bounded before it is applied. `Signature::validate` range-checks the block size, and the new `Signature::validate_for_base` applies the hash-count bound, which needs the base length.
- F-M-SUP M-12 part A: **done**, e663358. Deadlines on the handshake, channel open, probe and upload; the pool slot is marked connecting, and waiters wait on a condvar with a deadline instead of on a lock; control-socket clients connect without blocking and time out after 5 s (new `Probe::Unresponsive`); the tray is bounded through `status_report` → `probe`.
- OPS-3: **done**, a80744c. Every `ssh_command` script is sent as `sh -c '<quoted>'`. The fake ssh in `tests/supervisor.rs` refuses an unwrapped command.
- OPS-4: **done**, 2d219b1. `-T`, `ForwardAgent=no`, `ForwardX11=no`, `ClearAllForwardings=yes`, `PermitLocalCommand=no` and `ConnectTimeout=20` are on every ssh command, and `docs/configuration.md` lists them.
- F-M-OUT (M-8 prune + relayed stderr): **done**, 67be704. Prune uses an allowlist and quoted exact paths. Relayed stderr is decoded lossily, escaped with `display_safe`, and relayed in pieces of at most 4 KiB.
- F-L-MISC L-9 + L-22: **done**, f897956. `Response::Scan` is validated like a reassembled snapshot. A failed upload reports the last lines of the remote's stderr, not "Broken pipe".
